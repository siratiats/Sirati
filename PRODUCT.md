# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- Arabic-speaking job seekers and early-career to mid-career professionals improving resumes for real job applications.
- Context: often under time pressure, unsure why a CV is rejected, and need practical guidance in clear Arabic.
- Core job-to-be-done: diagnose CV weaknesses against ATS expectations and produce a stronger, application-ready CV.

## Product Purpose

Sirati helps users evaluate and improve their CV quality with actionable ATS-focused guidance, then generate a more professional CV version they can submit with confidence.

Success looks like:
- Users can complete analysis quickly with clear next actions.
- Recommendations are practical and immediately editable.
- Users leave with a more professional CV and higher confidence to apply.

## Brand Personality

- Useful
- Amazing
- Practical

Voice and tone:
- Clear, direct, supportive, and outcome-focused.
- Prefer practical guidance over marketing-heavy language.

## Anti-references

- Overly playful or gimmicky interfaces that reduce trust.
- Visual decoration that distracts from task completion.

## Design Principles

1. Clarity before flair: every screen should make the next action obvious.
2. Actionable over abstract: insights must translate into concrete CV improvements.
3. Confidence through evidence: scoring and recommendations should feel structured and explainable.
4. Task flow first: reduce friction from input to improved output.
5. Arabic-first usability: RTL readability and language quality are non-negotiable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target: WCAG AA baseline.
- Maintain strong text contrast and visible focus states across all controls.
- Motion should be moderate by default and respect reduced-motion user preferences.
- Keep language plain and concise for users with varying writing confidence.
