About us

An online platform dedicated to your career growth

Siratie is an online platform for career development. We offer a complete set of services that combine smart technology with a real understanding of the job market in Saudi Arabia and the Gulf. We believe every professional deserves professional tools that open doors.

Siratie was built to close the gap between job seekers and today's hiring requirements, where many employers rely on applicant tracking systems to read and filter CVs. Siratie is owned and operated by Digital Pass Establishment, registered in Saudi Arabia and based in Dammam, and we comply with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).
