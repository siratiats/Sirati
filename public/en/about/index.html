Our Mission & Technological Standards

Siratie was engineered to bridge the gap between talented job seekers and the modern algorithmic hiring standards prevalent across Saudi Arabia and the GCC. Today, major public entities and multinational employers rely heavily on Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to filter resumes before human review.

Our mission is to equip job seekers with intelligent tools that eliminate formatting disqualifications, provide standardized single-column templates, and uphold rigorous privacy standards aligned with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL).

ATS CV Analyzer

Heuristic algorithms that audit section hierarchy, font consistency, keyword coverage, and parsing integrity.

Bilingual CV Builder

An intuitive builder crafted for Arabic and English resumes, outputting standard PDFs with zero layout flaws.

Curated Gulf Job Feeds

Real-time aggregation of verified career opportunities across Saudi Arabia and the broader Gulf region.

Data Security & PDPL

Strict compliance with Saudi privacy legislation, encrypted data transmission, and autonomous one-click account deletion.
