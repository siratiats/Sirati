1. Instant deletion in the app

If you have the Siratie app installed on your device (Android or iOS), you can delete your account immediately:

  • Open the Siratie app and sign in to your account.
  • Go to Settings, scroll to Danger zone, and tap Delete account.
  • Verify your identity by entering your password, or by re-authenticating with Google or Apple if you signed up with a social account.
  • Confirm permanent deletion in the dialog — your account and data are erased immediately.

2. Request account deletion without the app

If you have uninstalled the app or can no longer access it, you can request permanent deletion of your account and data via email:

  • Send an email from the email address registered to your Siratie account to info@siratie.com.
  • Use the subject line Siratie Account Deletion Request and include your registered email address.
  • Our support team will verify ownership of the registered email address, permanently delete your account and associated data, and send you a confirmation email once completed.

Note: You can also delete individual CVs directly from the app or request partial data deletion via the same email without deleting your entire account.

3. Data permanently deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently erased and cannot be recovered:

  • Account and profile information: name, email address, mobile number, city, target job title, and profile photo.
  • Generated CVs and PDF files: all CVs you created and their exported PDF files stored on our servers.
  • ATS CV analyses: all CV analysis records and uploaded CV content associated with your account.
  • Notifications and device tokens: in-app notification history and push notification tokens (FCM).
  • Sessions and linked social logins: all active access tokens and linked Google / Apple sign-in associations (including revoking Sign in with Apple tokens).

4. Data that may be retained

We do not retain your CVs or profile data after account deletion. Only the following limited records may remain for legal or operational reasons:

  • Payment and subscription records: payments are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. Billing records are retained by the respective store and as required by applicable Saudi tax and financial regulations; we never store your card details.
  • Encrypted server backups: routine encrypted database backups rotate automatically and are purged within a maximum of 30 days.
  • Anonymised technical crash logs: Sentry error logs automatically mask personal data and cannot be linked back to your identity after deletion.

5. Processing timeline

  • In-app deletion: processed immediately upon confirmation; active account data is erased right away.
  • Email / web deletion request: verified and completed within 7 business days (and no later than 30 days under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law, PDPL), with a confirmation email sent upon completion.
  • Backup purge: any encrypted backup copies are automatically purged within 30 days.

6. Active subscriptions

Deleting your Siratie account does not automatically cancel paid subscriptions managed by the app stores. If you have an active subscription, please cancel it before deleting your account:

  • Android (Google Play): Google Play Store → Profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Siratie → Cancel subscription.
  • iOS (App Store): Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions → Siratie → Cancel Subscription.
