ATS Screening Reality

What Is an ATS and Why Do Resumes Get Rejected by Recruiter Filters?

Over 90% of Fortune 500 enterprises and top employers across Saudi Arabia and the GCC use Applicant Tracking Systems to filter out hundreds of candidates before a recruiter ever reviews an application.

Complex Columns & Tables

Multi-column designs, text boxes, and complex tables break text extraction pipelines, resulting in scrambled data or automatic disqualification.

Missing Target Keywords

If your resume omits essential industry skills mentioned in the job requisition, the scoring algorithm assigns your profile a low relevance score.

Non-Standard Section Headers

Creative headings confuse machine parsers. Standard headings like Work Experience, Education, and Skills guarantee correct categorization.
