Our commitment

We protect your personal data in line with the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), issued by Royal Decree No. (M/19) and amended by Royal Decree No. (M/148), and its implementing regulations.

The data controller is Digital Pass Establishment, registered in Saudi Arabia under CR No. 7051100811 and based in Dammam.

This policy covers the website (siratie.com) and the Siratie app on iOS and Android, including CV checking, CV building and job browsing.

Data minimisation: we collect only the minimum data needed to provide the service and never use it for unrelated purposes.

Data we collect

  • Identity and contact: name, email address, mobile number (optional) and city.
  • CV content: job title, qualifications, experience, skills, languages and certificates you enter or upload (PDF / Word).
  • Technical data: IP address, device and operating system, notification token, and crash logs to improve stability.
  • Subscription data: subscription status and verification through RevenueCat. We never store your card numbers or banking details; payments go through the official app stores.

How we use your data

We process your data on the basis of your consent and to provide the service you asked for, in order to:

  • check your CV against applicant tracking systems (ATS) and find missing keywords;
  • create and format your CV in Arabic and English and export it as a PDF;
  • show jobs that fit your career and send the alerts you choose;
  • provide support, answer your questions and improve the service.

We do not sell your data: we never sell or rent your personal data or CV content to anyone for advertising or marketing.

Service providers (processors)

We work with a small number of providers bound to protect your data:

  • AI providers (Anthropic, OpenAI and DeepInfra): to analyse CVs and suggest wording and keywords; your data is not used to train their models.
  • RevenueCat: to verify and renew subscriptions, without access to payment details.
  • Firebase (Google): to deliver notifications.
  • Sentry: to track technical errors, with sensitive data masked automatically.

Retention and account deletion

  • Guests without an account: a CV checked without signing up is kept for at most 24 hours to show the result, then deleted automatically.
  • Account holders: we keep your CVs while your account is active so you can edit and export them.
  • Deleting your account: you can permanently delete your account and all your data at any time in the app (Settings → Account → Delete account), or by emailing info@siratie.com. Deletion permanently erases your CVs, files and account data.

Your rights

Under the PDPL you may at any time exercise the right to:

  • Be informed of the legal basis for collecting your data and how it is processed.
  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct or complete your data directly in the app.
  • Erasure of your data once the purpose of collecting it has ended.
  • Withdraw consent or turn off notifications at any time.

For the terms that govern the service, see our Terms & Conditions.
