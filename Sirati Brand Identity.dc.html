sirati — Brand Identity System
Backend · Mobile · v1.0

1. Direction Summary

  • Name meaning drives the mark. Sirati (سيرتي, "my CV/my path") is a CV-improvement product for Arabic-speaking job seekers — the identity should visualize progress and a passed check, not a generic tech logo.
  • Confidence through evidence. Every mark reads as a completed, structured action (a check, an ascent) — never decorative flourish — matching the product's ATS-scoring, evidence-based tone.
  • One mark, two contexts. Backend and Mobile share one symbol and one color system; geometry of the container (chip vs. squircle) is the only differentiator — same DNA, instantly distinguishable in a stack.
  • Bilingual by construction, not by translation. The system ships a Latin lockup (Manrope) and an Arabic lockup (Tajawal) as equal, native-feeling siblings — not a Latin mark with Arabic bolted on.
  • Restraint at every size. Two brand colors, one geometric mark, monoline construction — built to survive a 16px favicon and a 20ft conference screen without redrawing.

2. Concept Family A — Ascent Mark SELECTED

Core symbol: a single monoline stroke that dips like a checkmark and rises into an ascending path, capped with a solid accent dot — reads simultaneously as "passed" and "moving forward." Fully custom, not a stock check icon.

Wordmark style: lowercase Manrope ExtraBold, tight tracking (-2%), paired with Tajawal Bold for سيرتي — both geometric, humanist sans with matching x-height weight.

Palette: Ink Navy #101B33 · Signal Teal #12B3A6 · Slate #5B6B82 · Mist #EEF2F6

Why it fits: the stroke is a literal "check" for Backend's job (ATS validation, scoring, pass/fail logic) and a literal "ascent" for Mobile's job (a user's CV visibly improving); one shape, two honest readings.

3. Concept Family B — Folded Page

Core symbol: a geometric document with a folded corner (the CV itself) with a check cut through its body — the most literal of the three families, still built from straight geometric strokes, no skeuomorphism.

Wordmark style: same Manrope / Tajawal pairing as Family A, set slightly wider to balance the boxier mark.

Palette: identical Ink Navy / Signal Teal system — only the symbol changes across families, keeping the palette question separate from the symbol question.

Why it fits: instantly legible as "your document" for Mobile's upload/edit flow; for Backend it reads as the data object the API processes — but the folded corner loses definition below ~20px and competes with real document/file icons in-product.

4. Concept Family C — Threaded Path

Core symbol: a single arch — two paths converging upward to one point — evoking "سيرة" (a path/journey) and a gateway toward a goal, capped with the same accent dot as a destination marker.

Wordmark style: Manrope / Tajawal, generously spaced to echo the mark's symmetry.

Palette: same Ink Navy / Signal Teal core.

Why it fits: strong metaphor for "your career path," but symmetric arches are a common fintech/data trope and the mark reads as static rather than active — it illustrates the name's meaning better than it illustrates the product's job (evaluate → fix → improve).

5. Final Direction Chosen

Family A — Ascent Mark is the production direction. It is the only concept that encodes both product jobs in one honest gesture (validate = check, improve = ascent) rather than illustrating the brand name literally. It survives 16px far better than Family B's folded corner, and it reads as active progress rather than Family C's static, over-used arch. Its monoline construction scales cleanly from a favicon to a splash screen without redraws, and its two-color system (one ink, one accent) keeps every future surface — Backend dashboards, Mobile screens, marketing — visually disciplined.

Sirati Mobile

sirati
MOBILE

Container: squircle (corner radius = 26% of side, iOS/Android superellipse-friendly), navy→teal diagonal gradient (#12384F → #0E8F86). Mark is full monochrome white here (stroke and dot) — the gradient itself carries the accent color, so the mark stays a single flat tone for maximum legibility on a moving/colored surface.

Clear Space, Minimum Size & Misuse

  • Clear space: keep empty space around the full lockup equal to the height of the accent dot (≈1 unit) on every side; nothing else may enter that margin.
  • Minimum size: mark-only down to 16px (favicon/tab); full lockup down to 96px wide on screen, 20mm in print.
  • Contrast floor: ink #101B33 on white = 15.8:1; white on ink = 15.8:1 — both exceed WCAG AAA for text. Teal is accent-only, never used as text on light backgrounds.
  • Don't recolor the stroke to anything outside Ink Navy / White / approved mono.
  • Don't separate the accent dot from the stroke or resize it independently.
  • Don't rotate, skew, mirror, or add a drop shadow to the mark.
  • Don't set the wordmark in any typeface other than Manrope (Latin) or Tajawal (Arabic).
  • Don't place the two-tone version on a busy photo — use mono white instead.

7. App Icon Specs — Sirati Mobile

1024px master
60pt @2x (120px)
24px
16px favicon
  • Icon-specific mark is re-drawn heavier than the wordmark lockup (stroke 13 vs. 9 on a 100-unit grid) — small-size legibility beats consistency with the print mark.
  • Full monochrome white on the navy→teal gradient — no two-tone dot at icon scale, it disappears below 40px.
  • Safe area: mark occupies the centre 66% of the canvas; iOS/Android both apply their own corner mask, so export the icon as a full square (no pre-rounded corners) at 1024×1024 and let each OS mask it.
  • Background never goes transparent — flat gradient fill only, per both platforms' opaque-icon requirement.
